Satisfactory Save & Recipes Guide

📂 LOCATION

Where to find your Save Games (.sav)

Satisfactory stores world saves locally on your Windows computer. To open the folder quickly:

1 Press Win + R on your keyboard to open Run
2 Paste the path below and press Enter:
STEAM %LOCALAPPDATA%\FactoryGame\Saved\SaveGames\<SteamID64>\
EPIC GAMES %LOCALAPPDATA%\FactoryGame\Saved\SaveGames\<EpicAccountID>\

💡 Select your latest .sav file (e.g. MyWorld.sav). You can also simply drag and drop the file directly onto this webpage!

⚡ SYNC

Alternate Recipes & M.A.M. Research

In Satisfactory, Alternate Recipes are unlocked by scanning Hard Drives in the M.A.M. (Molecular Analysis Machine). By loading your save file:

  • ⭐
    Unlocked Recipes ([ALT]): Recipes you have already unlocked in your save are immediately highlighted and available for planning.
  • 🔒
    Locked Recipes ([LOCKED]): Recipes you have not unlocked in that world are marked as locked.
  • 👁️
    "Hide recipes locked in save" Toggle: Easily filter the dropdowns to only display alternate recipes you can actually build right now!
  • 🛡️
    100% Client-Side & Private: The save parsing runs strictly in your web browser. No data or files are ever sent to any external server.
